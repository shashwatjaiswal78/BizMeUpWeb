## The challenge

Kutsu sells footwear from its store in Lucknow and wanted to sell online with the same care. Standard themes looked like every other store and didn’t fit the brand.

## The strategy

Use Shopify for what it does best, including checkout, payments and inventory, and custom-code the storefront so the design is entirely Kutsu’s own.

## The build

An e-commerce store on Shopify with a custom theme written in Liquid. Shoppers can jump straight into categories, see best sellers, buy a featured product without leaving the home page, read customer reviews and find the physical store.
