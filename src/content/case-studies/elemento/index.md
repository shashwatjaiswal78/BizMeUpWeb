## The challenge

Elemento is a home finishing showroom in Gorakhpur covering everything from floors and ceilings to kitchens and wellness. Customers had to visit in person to see which brands it carried and what it had done.

## The strategy

Bring the showroom online. Show the brands and the portfolio up front so visitors arrive already interested, and turn that interest into enquiries and showroom visits.

## The build

A website showcasing Elemento’s brands, product collections and portfolio, with a lead capture form and a “Book a Showroom Visit” call to action on every page.
